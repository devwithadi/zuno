# Zuno product context

## Platform

adaptive

## Purpose and users

Zuno is an open-source reading app for people following AI, technology,
business and markets. It runs on Android, iOS and web from one Expo client.
Readers can browse as guests or sign in through Clerk.

## Content

Public, free source endpoints feed a five-minute collection workflow.
Cards provide concise, cited points and links to original sources.
Accuracy, plain language and useful compression take priority over wit.
Extractive mode must identify source excerpts honestly. Images come from
supplied evidence; missing cover images must never be fabricated news photos.

## Confirmed design direction

The user chose Apple-inspired light surfaces, soft depth and airy type
on October 27, 2026, with subtle texture, a modern Gen Z tone, and rich content.
TypeUI's public prompt examples are a reference for readable layouts.

## Constraints

Preserve guest reading, topic filtering, refresh, pagination and citations.
Every authored code file has at most 100 physical lines. Prompts stay in prompts/.
Personalization, saved stories and notifications are outside the current MVP.
